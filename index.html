Why Calla exists

You're navigating
this in a system
not built for you.

The NHS treats same-sex couples as an afterthought. Fertility apps assume intercourse. Sperm banks have no neutral comparison. Legal rights differ by route and nobody explains them. And the internet's advice was written for someone else.

Calla is the place queer couples on the journey to motherhood come to find each other, share real experiences, and access the information that should have been there from the start.

What Calla is

Community first.
Tools built in.
All for you.

🌸

TTC Community

Connect with other lesbian and queer couples at exactly your stage — same route, same city, same questions. Not a general forum. Your people.

🗺

NHS Postcode Navigator

Enter your postcode. Instantly see what your ICB funds for same-sex couples, what you need to prove, and how to challenge anything unfair.

🧬

Donor Bank Comparison

Filter across Cryos, London Sperm Bank, and European Sperm Bank in one neutral interface — by ethnicity, open ID, health profile, and personality.

📅

Insemination-First Tracking

Log IUI attempts, BBT, LH tests, and clinic appointments. Cycle predictions calibrated for donor sperm timing — not intercourse.

💬

Shared Experiences

Real stories from queer parents on every route — IUI, IVF, reciprocal IVF, home insemination. What worked, what didn't, what they wish they'd known.

🔀

Pathway Decision Tool

IUI, IVF, reciprocal IVF, or home insemination? Ten questions. A personalised recommendation with costs, success rates, and what each route means for both of you.

⚖️

Legal Rights by Route

Know your parental rights before you start — by conception method, donor type, and jurisdiction. No surprises after the fact.

🏥

Queer-Rated Clinics

Clinic ratings from real same-sex patients — not self-declared "LGBTQ+ friendly" badges. Find care that genuinely understands your journey.
