NHS Access Gap

The system wasn't
designed for you.

Queer couples in the UK face a structurally unequal NHS fertility access pathway. These aren't edge cases — they're policy.

Stat 01

NHS Funding Gap

16%

of same-sex female couples received NHS funding for their first IVF treatment, compared to 52% of opposite-sex couples aged 18–39.

HFEA Family Formations, 2024
Stat 02

Three Times Less Likely

3×

Opposite-sex couples were three times as likely to receive NHS funding than same-sex female couples — for the same treatment.

HFEA, 2024
Stat 03

The Postcode Lottery

4/42

Only 4 of 42 Integrated Care Boards in England have implemented equal IVF access for same-sex couples, despite government commitments made in 2022.

NHS England · OVUM, 2025
Stat 04

The Self-Fund Barrier

Female same-sex couples must self-fund 6–12 rounds of IUI — costing up to £50,000 — just to prove they qualify for the NHS IVF pathway that heterosexual couples access freely.

GOV.UK NICE Guidelines, 2025
Stat 05

Best Outcomes, Least Support

43%

Female same-sex couples have the highest birth rate per embryo transferred (43%) of any patient group — yet receive the lowest NHS funding.

HFEA, 2022
Stat 06

Patient Dissatisfaction

The HFEA 2024 National Patient Survey found higher dissatisfaction among same-sex and gender-diverse couples — specifically on quality of information and coordination of care.

HFEA Patient Survey, 2024
Competitive Landscape

What exists
vs what
you actually need.

The market has fragments. Queer parents TTC currently use 5–8 separate tools — none of which talk to each other, and none built with you in mind.

✓ Tools that exist

  • 📱Clue / Fertility Friend — cycle tracking with some inclusive language
  • 📚GWK Academy — education for gay men (US-centric, web only)
  • 🧬Cryos / London Sperm Bank — individual donor bank websites
  • 💼Carrot Fertility — employer fertility benefits (B2B, not consumer)
  • 🔗Pride Angel / Modamily — known donor matching platforms
  • 📖OVUM / LGBT Mummies — UK information blogs and guides
  • 🏥HFEA website — clinic register (complex, clinical, hard to navigate)
