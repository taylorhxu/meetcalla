About Calla

Built by the
community it
belongs to.

Calla exists because we looked for the community we needed when we were trying to conceive — and it wasn't there. So we decided to build it.

The journey to motherhood as a queer couple is extraordinary. It's also lonely, expensive, confusing, and stacked against you by a system that still treats heterosexual couples as the default.

The NHS makes same-sex couples jump through hoops that straight couples never face. You can spend £50,000 proving you're infertile before you access the same treatment your neighbours get for free. Only 4 of 42 Integrated Care Boards in England have implemented equal access, despite commitments made in 2022.

And when you go looking for support — for real experiences, real information, real community — you find fertility forums full of people whose journey looks nothing like yours, apps that assume intercourse, and sperm bank websites that all look like they were designed in 2003.

Calla is built on a different starting point: queer couples trying to conceive deserve a home. Not a retrofitted tool. Not a subforum. A real community, built from the ground up for the people who need it.

A place where you can find someone who's six months ahead of you on the same route. Where the ICB information for your postcode is one search away. Where you can compare donors across every UK bank without visiting five different websites. Where someone will tell you what reciprocal IVF actually feels like, not just what it is.

Calla is that place. We're building it in London, for the UK first. We're building with our community, shaped by the people who know this journey from the inside.

What we stand for

Our values aren't
decoration.

🌸

Community First

Calla is a community that happens to have great tools — not a tool that tacked on a forum. The people you meet here matter as much as the information.

🌈

Queer-First, Always

We design from queer experiences outward. Every default, every label, every journey is built for lesbian and queer couples — not retrofitted for them.

📊

Evidence-Based

We cite our sources. Every statistic comes from HFEA, NICE, ONS, or peer-reviewed research. Real information, not reassurance.

🔒

Privacy-First

Your fertility data is among the most sensitive information you have. We will never sell it, share it, or use it for advertising. Your data is yours.

⚖️

Radical Accessibility

Fertility inequality is compounded by class. Core features of Calla will always be free. We're also building grant and finance navigation for queer families.

🌍

Intersectional by Design

Race, ethnicity, trans identity, disability, and income all shape the TTC experience. Calla is built for the whole queer community — not just one part of it.
